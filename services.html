Aftercare

Good ink and clean piercings heal well — but only if you look after them. Follow these basics and your work will age beautifully.

01

Keep it clean

Wash fresh tattoos gently with unscented soap; clean new piercings with sterile saline twice daily.

02

Hands off

No picking, scratching or twisting. Touch healing work only with freshly washed hands.

03

Moisturize light

Thin layer of recommended ointment on tattoos once peeling starts — never smother it.

04

Stay out of water

No pools, hot tubs, lakes or long soaks for at least two weeks on tattoos; four on piercings.

05

Block the sun

Keep fresh work out of direct sun while healing. Once healed, sunscreen keeps tattoos bold for years.

06

Ask us anytime
