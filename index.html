Our story

Broadway ink,
since 2009

Sol Tribe has been tattooing and piercing on Broadway in Denver since 2009 — over fifteen years of custom pieces, blackwork, fine line and professional piercings for the neighborhood we call home.

The studio runs on a simple idea: every piece is designed around you. Custom tattoos start with a free sit-down consultation, flash comes straight off the wall, and piercings are done with implant-grade jewelry and sterile single-use tools — every time, no shortcuts.
